Delete Your Zawada Account

Account & data deletion requests

Last updated: August 26, 2026

If you created an account in the Zawada mobile app and would like to delete your account and associated personal data, you can submit a request using the instructions below.

How to request deletion

Send an email to our support team at alaayedamer@gmail.com or contact us by phone / WhatsApp at 0936437002. Use the subject line: Zawada Account Deletion Request.

Please include in your message:

  • The email address or phone number linked to your Zawada account
  • Your full name (as registered in the app)
  • A brief note that you want your account deleted

An administrator will review and process your request. We may contact you to verify your identity before deletion is completed.

What will be deleted

When your account is deleted, we will remove or anonymize, where applicable:

  • Your account profile (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Profile photo and uploaded documents
  • Authentication credentials associated with your account
  • Personal data linked to your account that is not required to be retained by law

What may be kept

Some information may be retained for a limited time when required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or accounting purposes — for example, completed order records that must be kept for compliance. When retention is required, data is kept only as long as necessary and is not used for marketing.

Processing time

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation by email once your request has been completed.

Questions

For privacy-related questions, see our Privacy Policy or contact alaayedamer@gmail.com or call / WhatsApp 0936437002.
