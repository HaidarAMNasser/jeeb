Last updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zawada ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By using Zawada, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who we are

App name: Zawada
Developer: Haidar Nasser
Contact email: haideramnasser09@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, password, and delivery address.
  • Profile and registration data: profile photo and documents you choose to upload (for example, delivery driver registration).
  • Order information: items ordered, order status, delivery details, and payment-related receipts you upload when required.
  • Location data: GPS location when you choose "Use my location", pick a delivery address on the map, or when delivery tracking is active.
  • Device and app data: device identifiers, push notification tokens, app version, and basic usage data needed to operate the service.
  • Communications: support messages or information you send to us.

3. How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Process orders and deliver food to your address.
  • Show maps, delivery tracking, and order status updates.
  • Send push notifications about orders and account activity.
  • Improve app performance, security, and customer support.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Permissions we request

  • Internet: to connect to our servers and load app content.
  • Location: to set delivery addresses and show live delivery tracking.
  • Notifications: to send order updates and alerts.
  • Photos / gallery: when you upload a profile picture, registration documents, or payment receipts.

5. Third-party services

We use trusted third-party providers that may process data on our behalf, including:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, realtime database, cloud messaging).
  • Google Maps (maps, location, and directions).
  • Our backend API servers (account, orders, and app data).

These providers have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only when necessary to:

  • Complete your order (for example, with restaurants, delivery partners, or payment processors).
  • Operate technical services (hosting, maps, notifications).
  • Meet legal requirements or protect users and our service.

7. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with the law. You may request deletion of your account by contacting us.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can update profile information inside the App.
  • You can disable location or notifications in your device settings (some features may not work).
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by emailing us.

10. Children's privacy

Zawada is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at the same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
